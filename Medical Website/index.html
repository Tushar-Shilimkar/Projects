Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order medicines via WhatsApp?
Simply fill out the form above or click "Order on WhatsApp", send your medicine list or a photo of your prescription. Our team will verify availability, calculate total cost with available discounts, and confirm delivery time!
What are the home delivery timings & charges?
Our home delivery operates daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM via Porter / Dunzo / Local Delivery Partners. Delivery charges are calculated based on actual distance and are payable by the customer upon delivery.
Are generic medicines safe and effective?
Yes! Generic medicines contain the exact same active ingredients, salt formula, and therapeutic efficacy as branded medicines, but cost 10% to 60% less. All generic medicines at Lifeline Medicare are sourced from certified manufacturers.
Is a prescription required for ordering medicines?
For prescription-only medicines (Schedule H & H1 drugs), a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory. You can easily attach photo of prescription in our WhatsApp order form.
